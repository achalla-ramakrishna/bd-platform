# Business context

## What CodeWalnut sells

- **Staff augmentation**: CodeWalnut engineers work as contractors inside a
  client's team, billed per engineer per month (or day or hour).
- **Outsourced delivery**: CodeWalnut builds software for a client under a
  scoped project, billed fixed-price or time-and-materials.

CodeWalnut has a handful of enterprise clients today. Their names are kept out
of this repository.

## The market

India has more than 2,000 GCCs (2,117 in FY26 per the Zinnov-Nasscom India GCC
Landscape Report), with Bengaluru the largest hub, followed by Pune,
Hyderabad, Delhi-NCR and Chennai. GCCs are hiring, are short of AI, data and
platform engineering skills, take more than 45 days to fill critical roles,
and are using more contractors (about 25% of GCC staffing in 2026). The
business requirements document has a market outlook for 2027–2028 with
sources.

GCCs buy engineering services through a few people: the centre head,
engineering and product leaders, talent acquisition, and vendor management or
procurement.

## The business problem

- **Reach**: knowing which of the 2,000+ centres fit CodeWalnut, and who decides
  there.
- **Follow-through**: working each account across LinkedIn, email, events and
  referrals until there is a deal, and having engineers ready when it closes.
- **Growth**: tracking deployed engineers, contract end dates and expansion
  opportunities inside existing clients.

## Starting point

There is no existing system. Clients, contracts, contacts and the engineers
currently deployed are kept in people's spreadsheets and heads. All starting
data is uploaded from spreadsheets (see [data-import](data-import/README.md)),
and the application becomes the system of record from then on.

About 10 people will use the application.

## Business objectives

| ID | Objective | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| OBJ-01 | Build a qualified target list | GCC accounts researched and scored for fit |
| OBJ-02 | Start more conversations | First meetings with decision makers per month |
| OBJ-03 | Win new clients | New clients signed (MSA or first SOW) |
| OBJ-04 | Grow existing clients | Billable engineers across all clients |
| OBJ-05 | Shorten time to deploy | Days from won requirement to engineer start |
| OBJ-06 | Keep the bench working | Share of engineers billable |
| OBJ-07 | Forecast revenue | Weighted pipeline against actual bookings |

Targets for each are set by leadership in the business requirements document.
